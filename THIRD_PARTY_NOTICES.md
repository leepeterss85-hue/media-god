## LibVLC Android

Media God's Android and Fire TV native apps include the VideoLAN LibVLC Android library as an optional compatibility playback engine. Media God continues to use AndroidX Media3 and the device's hardware decoders first; LibVLC is invoked only when the normal native decoder reports an unsupported codec/container/audio path.
